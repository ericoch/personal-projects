How to play
A hidden species is chosen from the tree of life. Guess any species — you'll be told the smallest named clade that contains both your guess and the hidden target (e.g. Mammalia, Carnivora, Felidae). Use the clue to narrow your search and work down the tree. Common names are shown alongside scientific names throughout. Use hints to reveal the next clade down if you get stuck.
Options
Extant species only Exclude extinct taxa from the random walk and reject extinct guesses
Species-only guesses Only accept guesses at species rank — not genera, families, etc.
Genus counts as win Guessing the correct genus of the target species is accepted as a victory

Powered by the Open Tree of Life, GBIF & OneZoom APIs
